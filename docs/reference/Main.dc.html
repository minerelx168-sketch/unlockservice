01 · Color tokens — light (:root)

Warm-neutral whites, deep teal ink.

Whites carry a faint teal cast (#f5fafa, #edf8f8) rather than pure grey. Text never goes black — the darkest ink is #0b2f3d.

03 · Type ramp

One family, weight 400–740, negative tracking at scale.

Google Sans Flex (self-hosted woff2), falling back to Google Sans → Arial. Headlines carry heavy negative letter-spacing (up to −0.058em) and line-height below 1.0; body sits at a generous 1.65.

04 · Shape, depth & layout

Big radii, hairline borders, one very wide shadow.
