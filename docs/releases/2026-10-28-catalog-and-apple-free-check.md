# Provider catalog and Apple Free Check

Owner-approved retail prices come from the uploaded CSV, recorded by provider ID and source SHA-256 in `catalog/owner-selling-prices-20261028.json`. The 129 current provider entries become 128 paid listings and Apple Free Check (214); eight historical entries absent from the provider list remain unavailable. Old orders and invoices retain their original prices.

Apple Basic Info is removed from paid checkout. The free endpoint requires an active, email-verified customer account and the exact sync mapping `check:apple_free -> 214`. An immediate database transaction reserves at most three attempts per account per UTC calendar day. An uncertain provider response consumes its reservation; replaying the same idempotency key never makes a second provider request. Free checks create no invoice, credit movement, or purchase event.

Service descriptions and 72 meaningful example outputs were reviewed against the provider's visible catalog on 2026-10-28. Example identifiers are redacted, source-language messages translated, and download links represented as placeholders. Empty or non-result placeholders are omitted. Explicit `Clean` values receive green styling; ambiguous numeric values are not converted into a positive verdict. Real paid results retain the original provider response through existing encrypted storage. Apple Free Check also retains the full provider output encrypted and reveals it only through the owner-scoped result view, including fields not recognized by the structured report parser.

IMEI, serial, combined IMEI/serial, phone, Case ID, ECID, UDID and unlock-number inputs use service-specific validation and authenticated encryption. Provider submission still follows confirmed payment or a successful credit hold. No paid provider request was made during release QA.

Operational exception: provider IDs 25 and 926 require owner information/Notes in their descriptions, while the provider service-list API declares no custom fields and the published API guide does not document Notes submission. Keep both retail listings visible but omit their checkout mappings until the provider confirms the API contract. The release enables 126 paid mappings plus Apple Free Check; it must not collect payment for these two unsupported workflows.
